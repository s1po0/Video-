# SliceVid Android design direction

SliceVid is a Kurdish, mobile-first utility for choosing and saving a video or a clip. Keep the existing dark, blue-leaning identity familiar, while removing the long one-page flow.

- Audience: phone users moving between a YouTube link, clip settings, subtitles and saved files.
- Palette: deep ink and slate surfaces, off-white text, one clear blue accent for the primary action and selected navigation.
- Typography: platform system font for Kurdish coverage; compact headings and readable form labels.
- Layout: four task-focused screens with persistent bottom navigation. A small video summary anchors editing; controls are grouped by the work they perform.
- Motif: a short, blue selection mark representing a clip boundary, repeated in the brand and active screen.
- Motion: only a short pressed-state response; respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

The dark surface preserves the identity of the installed app and separates a video thumbnail from form controls. The blue accent indicates the current task and the action that advances it. The fixed navigation keeps the four tasks reachable without a long scroll.
