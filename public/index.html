Frequently asked questions

What is SliceVid?

SliceVid is a free online tool to cut, trim and download any part of a YouTube video as MP4, WebM or MP3, and to download subtitles.

How do I cut and download part of a YouTube video?

Paste the video link, drag the timeline handles to your start and end times, pick a format, and hit “Download clip”.

Can I download a YouTube clip as MP4 or WebM?

Yes. SliceVid exports trimmed clips as MP4 or WebM video, or as MP3 audio only.

Can I download subtitles?

Yes. Choose a language and format (SRT or VTT) and SliceVid downloads the captions — including auto-generated ones when available.

Do I need an account?

No. SliceVid works without sign-up. Just paste a link and download.

Does SliceVid add a watermark?

No. Your clips are downloaded clean, with no watermark added.
